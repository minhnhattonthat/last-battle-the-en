# Style reference

How the merged English reads; follow it in fixes and later passes. Binding names: `glossary.md`. Rulings: `docs/decisions.md`.

- **Format:** speaker pages `Name{nl}"speech`; `"` opens speech, never closes; plain ASCII; no em dashes; `...` for
  JP `・・・`; `!?` kept as written.
- **Honorifics (D1):** kept where the JP uses them (Yuri-sama, Febennels-sama, Kurt-sama); `{actor}さん` in shop
  templates drops it.
- **Voices:**
  - Kurt's elders and Febennels: measured, slightly old-fashioned ("shall", "listen well").
  - Mei: quick and blunt with Kurt ("this guy").
  - Borg, sailors, Yaak fighters, thugs: rough contractions ("gonna", "Heh heh", "Curses!").
  - Guld officers: formal and stiff ("You are a traitor against our Guld kingdom").
  - Shopkeepers: top14 casual ("What'll you buy?"), top15 servile-polite ("A customer of taste!").
  - Beebo: chatty, "Kee kee!".
- **Chants** are Swedish-flavoured nonsense: romanised, dotted pauses ("Vatten... Bleckfel... Reka!").
- **Names:** spells 8 letters, items/tactics up to 12 (D10); currency "Sol".
